    Firmware

    The editor needs the MIDI Muppet config firmware on the Arduino. Install it once – it works for Arduino Nano and Pro Mini with ATmega168 or ATmega328P, in the 4btn and in the original MIDI Muppet HX.

    How it works
    
      
      
        
    1. Connect the Arduino in the pedal to the computer with a USB cable (a Pro Mini through its USB–serial adapter, with DTR connected).
      2. 
        
    2. First time: click Install firmware and pick the Arduino's port. Your old sketch is replaced.
      3. 
        
    3. Click Connect pedal. The pedal restarts and blinks green/red while it is in setup mode.
      4. 
        
    4. Click a switch in the picture, choose what a short and a long press should do, then Save to pedal.
      5. 
        
    5. Disconnect, plug in the MIDI cable and play. Settings stay on the pedal.
      6. 
      
    
      
    Details for the curious
        
    After power-up the firmware listens on USB for 1.5 seconds before it starts as a foot switch, so it doesn't send anything to your other pedals while it listens. A switch without a long press action reacts as soon as you step on it; with a long press action, the short press is sent when you lift your foot. In the HX Stomp profile, switches 1 and 2 work exactly like the original MIDI Muppet HX: scroll, footswitch, snapshot, looper and tuner modes, and holding both switches changes mode.
    
        
    Wiring: switches on D2, D3, D6, D8 to ground (the original MIDI Muppet HX uses only D2 = UP and D3 = DN); bicolor LED green on D4, red on D5; MIDI out on TX. See the project on GitHub.
